Lecture: Epistemology & Decision Systems

Oct 24, 2025 • 4 min read • Seminar Audio

Summary

2 points

Empiricist epistemologies inherently falter when treating sensory correlation as deterministic causal truth .

Humean induction demonstrates that historical frequency provides probabilistic bias rather than formal logical necessity .

Key Concepts

3 definitions

Classical Foundationalism

The doctrine that epistemic justification depends upon a foundation of basic, non-inferential perceptual premises resisting further rational regressive inquiry.

Problem of Induction

Projection of constant conjunction into universal physical law without deductive warrant, mistaking epistemic habit for ontological invariability.

Heuristic Decoupling

The operational mandate to strictly isolate Bayesian posterior probability distributions from psychological conviction inside machine-augmented inference loops.
